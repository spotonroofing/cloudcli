# Motion

- Status motion (ui12 job 8): the app's working spinner is the `TodoStatusIcon` partial-circle arc rotated by the CSS `spinner-ramp` animation (`.animate-spinner-ramp` in `src/index.css` — 1.2s eased revolutions that ramp up and settle, transform-only, `animation: none` under reduced motion; CSS-driven so the computed animation stays running independent of renders). In the jobs view the working task row and the active job's icon breathe via `.animate-row-breathe` (opacity-only pulse of the white, 1 to 0.65 — never scale or size, ui14 job 2; reduced-motion aware). When a job's status lands on completed while mounted, a `sweepOnComplete` icon plays a full-circle stroke sweep (`data-slot="status-sweep"`, pathLength draw then fade) and the checkmark draws in after it; rows that mount already completed render the settled check with no sweep, and under reduced motion (re-read from `matchMedia` at transition time) the sweep is skipped entirely. The active job's segmented ring pulses its working segment with the white glow (`.animate-segment-glow`, ui13 job 7) — opacity swell plus a white drop-shadow on the same 2.4s beat as `row-breathe` so ring and row swell together; static full-ink under reduced motion.
- Motion laws (ui13 job 7): every animation ramps — eased in AND out on the vendored beUI curves (`src/shared/view/beui/ease.ts`: `EASE_OUT` cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1) for entrances/reveals, `EASE_IN_OUT` cubic-bezier(0.77,0,0.175,1) for drawers and sheets, or the exported springs) — never `linear`, never the weak browser defaults (`ease`/`ease-in`/`ease-out`), never an abrupt ending. Drawers and sheets travel on `EASE_IN_OUT` at 0.3-0.32s in both directions (SidebarFooterDrawer, SidebarSurface, the mobile sidebar drawer). Every popout — dropdown menus, popovers, tooltips, hand-rolled modals — opens with the one growing entrance: the CSS `popout-enter` clip-wipe grow in `src/index.css` (`popout-enter-up` when it opens above its trigger; `popout-enter-center` scale for flex-centered modal panels only — it animates transform, so never on translate-positioned elements; `overlay-enter` for scrims), sourced from the beUI AgentDisclosure wipe on `EASE_OUT`; the shared `Dialog` keyframes (`tailwind.config.js`) carry the same curve. New animation work picks its curve from `ease.ts` or these classes; ad-hoc cubic-beziers and default eases are a review failure. Reduced motion is law: the CSS classes collapse under the global reduce rule, and every motion/react animation must gate on `useReducedMotion`.
- Seasonal touches live in one date-gated, pointer-inert layer at the app-shell integration point. They use theme ink at low opacity, keep phone safe areas clear, and become static under `prefers-reduced-motion`; new seasonal work extends that component instead of scattering calendar checks through the UI.
- Loading placeholders (ui15 job 13) use only the shared content-shaped `Skeleton` pulse. Its Tailwind pulse curve ramps opacity in and out, and `motion-reduce:animate-none` leaves the held shape static; surfaces do not add independent loader animation.
- Thinking-grid motion (ui15 job 22): every named `PixelLoader` pattern uses `bui-pixel-on`, with a dim 0.15 resting state and the shared strong in-out ramp for its wavefront. Status pair changes use one loader: grid and word fade out together over 220ms on the same ramp, swap while transparent, and fade back in. Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, cell animation and the swap transition are both disabled; the nine cells remain at rest and pair changes are immediate.

## Performance budgets

- Measure the real dev instance with `node scripts/perf/check-app-performance.mjs --agent-session <authenticated-agent-browser-session> --dev-origin http://127.0.0.1:4748 --mode verify --out-dir <artifact-directory>`. The script applies 4x CPU throttling, records Chrome traces plus React Profiler component tables, and exercises five seconds idle, three real chat switches, five-second pointer/wheel sweeps over jobs and the sidebar, 30 heap switches, the selection spring, and the animation audit.
- At 4x CPU, idle, the jobs sweep, and the sidebar sweep allow no task over 50ms; the slowest of three chat switches must paint its transcript in under 150ms; retained heap after 30 switches must stay within 20% of its collected start. The selection dot must expose at least three distinct transform samples during each roughly 300ms hop. Hidden-tab and offscreen animation counts, and layout-affecting keyframe counts, must all be zero.
- Long-list rows are memoized or otherwise scoped, and closed drawers do not keep their full descendants mounted. Polls preserve state identity when payloads are equal; changed long-history snapshots reconcile at concurrent priority. Realtime frames update only the session they name. Timers are shared per cadence and stop without subscribers or while the document is hidden. Motion remains transform/opacity-only, does no layout read during render, and the app-level animation budget pauses animated elements outside the viewport or in a hidden document.
